Our Story

Where Heritage Meets Modern Elegance

Founded in Jaipur, Veloura was created out of a deep reverence for India's handloom traditions. We work directly with over 1,200 master artisan families across Kanchipuram, Varanasi, Mysore, and Bengal.

Every saree and ensemble is hand-woven using 100% certified pure Mulberry silk and real zari threads — creating wearable heirlooms to be cherished today and passed down to future generations.
